01 CHAPTER ONE • REASONING & REFLECTION

The Trajectory of Deliberation.

For decades, language modeling operated under a rigid, ungrounded paradigm: token prediction conditioned strictly on static prefix history. This open-loop behavior yielded fluent outputs that decoupled catastrophically from factual reality when confronted with multi-step logical requirements. The fundamental thesis of modern agentic literature posits that reasoning must not occur in an observational vacuum.

In the watershed paper ReAct [Yao et al., 2022], the authors demonstrated that interleaving reasoning traces ("Thought") with discrete executable commands ("Action") directly bridges internal policy formation with intermediate observation returns. By grounding the deliberation loop at every step, error propagation dropped by over 34% on multi-hop benchmarks.

“Thinking without acting invites hallucination; acting without thinking breeds random drift.”

Subsequent frameworks evolved this feedback loop from single-turn reactive triggers into deliberate search spaces. In Tree of Thoughts [Yao et al., 2023], problem-solving is reformulated as tree search with heuristics, allowing language models to deliberately backtrack, self-evaluate intermediate hypotheses, and look ahead across complex game-of-24 problem sets.
